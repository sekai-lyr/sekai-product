ART DIRECTION
Avant-garde editorial design. Modernist graphic design, structuralism, constructivism,
experimental art magazine, art-exhibition key visual, concept-design archive sheet.
Cool, restrained, conceptual, experimental, strongly structural, high art direction.
This is NOT a commercial anime promotional image.

SUBJECT
The subject is a deconstructed anime figure — 朝比奈真冬.

DECOMPOSITION MANDATE
Do NOT render an ordinary complete full-body character illustration.
Do NOT stand the character in front of a decorative background.
Treat the figure as raw material for the layout: crop the figure at two or more frame edges; show a magnified facial or eye slice as an inset window; offset a ghosted duplicate by a few millimetres; separate the line drawing from its colour block and shift them apart; cut the silhouette with the grid so the background shows through the body; render part of the body as pure flat plane and part as bare line art.
It may appear incomplete, cropped, transparent, repeated, fragmented or partially erased.
Recognisability must survive through: a spell-circuit diagram and glyph ring used as construction geometry.

VISUAL MOTIFS — abstract design language derived from the subject. Never literal symbols.
  1. a muted waveform flattened to near silence; thin ruled staves; the figure rendered as an unlit silhouette inside a lit system
  2. a fracture diagram: one plate broken along annotated seams
  3. a fanned document block at the margin, tabs aligned to the grid
  4. a descending archive stack; tomographic strata below the fold

FIGURE / GROUND FUSION
extend the hair, clothing or weapon beyond the body as structural line work that continues into the layout grid; let garment planes bleed out into the background colour fields; dissolve the lower body into paper grain.
There must be no separation between figure and ground — they are one printed system,
sharing the same grid, the same tone, the same paper.

LAYOUT
16:9 wide format. Asymmetric composition, generous negative space. The figure sits
RIGHT of frame — never centred, never in a normal poster pose. Build a clear horizontal
visual flow across the frame. Use: a non-symmetrical modular grid, hairline frames,
coordinate points, tick marks, a proportional scale bar, module rectangles, diagonal
construction lines, transparent overlays, collage edges cut by the frame,
one magnified inset window, and an archive data block.

COLOUR
Restrained, desaturated, high-end. Base: charcoal, ash-grey, cold off-white.
One accent only: deep aubergine ink-violet (#4C3A5E). The accent touches ONLY the key nodes — part of the
figure, the eye, an ability trace, one graphic module, the archive numbering, a
hairline, or the title mark. No cheap neon, no large saturated fields, no glow bloom.

MATERIAL / TEXTURE
aged paper fibre with ink diffusion, photocopier noise at the edges.
